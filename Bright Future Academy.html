About us

A school where every child is known

Purnima School combines a strong academic base with sport, music and values education. Small classes mean each student gets real attention from their teacher.

  • ✓ Qualified, caring faculty
  • ✓ Smart classrooms, science and computer labs
  • ✓ Safe campus with school bus routes
  • ✓ Sports, music, art and student clubs
